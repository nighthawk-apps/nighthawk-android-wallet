Nighthawk Wallet is a work-in-progress non-custodial Android wallet built on DarkFi-oriented architecture. Native sync, transfers, and daemon RPC are staged behind an explicit Kotlin façade until UniFFI/`darkfid` backing ships. Note: This is currently a `-test` release intended for F-Droid and development preview.

What you get today
- Self-custodial 22-word DarkFi recovery phrase backup (PDF export supported).
- A 1:1 privacy-first architecture: Your wallet generates a single DarkFi address from your phrase.
- Navigation surfaces for transfers (mock until JNI lands), receive, settings, and experimental DarkIRC-aligned chat diagnostics.

Important limitations
- Integrated exchange, card-on-ramp, referral flows, and vendor-specific funding shortcuts were intentionally removed.
- Chat performs Kotlin-side seed/Tor diagnostics only until the Rust `darkirc` stack from DarkFi `bin/` is JNI-linked.
- Fiat hints rely on public spot APIs (`darkfi` slug on CoinGecko today—confirm before relying on numbers).

Operational notes
- Wallet networking targets configurable DarkFi-style RPC endpoints (`darkfid`-style host/port); defaults point at loopback / emulator-friendly addresses for development.
- Store URLs in README/marketing materials are placeholders until listings go live.
