Nighthawk is a self-custodial, privacy-first wallet built on DarkFi. Your keys, your coins — no intermediaries.

Features
- Self-custodial 22-word recovery phrase with PDF backup
- Native DarkFi wallet sync via Rust core (darkfid RPC)
- Send and receive DRK with full privacy
- DAO governance hub — browse DAOs and proposals on-chain
- Experimental DarkIRC-aligned encrypted chat
- Configurable DarkFi RPC endpoints
- Fiat price hints via public APIs

Privacy by default
- Single DarkFi address derived from your recovery phrase
- No analytics, no tracking, no third-party services
- All wallet data stays on your device

Note
This is a work-in-progress release. Some features are still under active development. Nighthawk is provided "as is", without warranty.
